Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version: the debts and payments you enter in ChipAway stay on your phone. We don't ask for your bank logins, we don't show ads, we don't track you, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

ChipAway is made by Peter Kennedy, a sole developer based in Liverpool, UK ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the limited data described below under UK data protection law. You can contact us at chipawayappsupport@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The information you enter into the app, such as your debts, balances, interest rates, payments, round-up history, streaks and reminder times, is stored only on your phone. We cannot see it and we do not receive a copy. If you delete the app, this information is deleted from your phone. If you back up your phone, Apple may include the app's data in your backup, according to your own Apple settings.

Screenshot upload (optional)

If you choose to upload a screenshot of your spending, the image is sent securely to our server. The server passes it to an AI service, which reads the transactions and sends the amounts back to the app. We do not keep your screenshot: it is deleted as soon as it has been read. We do not keep the transactions on our server either, as they are returned to your phone.

A screenshot can contain personal details such as shop names or account information. We recommend cropping it to just the transactions before you upload it. Our AI provider processes the image only to return the result to us, under its commercial terms, which do not allow your data to be used to train its models. Uploading a screenshot is always your choice, and the rest of the app works without it.

Subscriptions and payments

ChipAway offers an optional paid subscription. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat, a subscription management service, to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status. It does not receive your name, your debts or your spending.

Reminders

The daily reminders and bank-transfer prompts are notifications created on your phone. They do not involve any server and no data is sent anywhere to produce them. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's Settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for, or store, your bank login details.
  • We do not connect to your bank accounts.
  • We do not show adverts or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

Who we share data with

Only the service providers needed to run the app: Apple (purchases), RevenueCat (subscription status), our AI provider (reading screenshots you choose to upload) and our server hosting provider. They act on our instructions and only receive what they need to do their job.

Our lawful reasons

We handle subscription status so that we can provide the app you have paid for (contract). We process screenshots you upload because you ask us to (consent), and you can stop at any time by not uploading. We keep basic technical logs to keep the service secure and working (legitimate interests).

How long we keep data

Screenshots are deleted as soon as they have been read. Subscription status is kept by RevenueCat while you are a customer, as described in its policy. Everything else stays on your phone under your control.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete any personal data we hold about you, object to how we use it, or ask us to restrict it. Contact us at chipawayappsupport@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. If you are not happy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

ChipAway is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Not financial advice

ChipAway is a budgeting and motivation tool. It does not give regulated financial advice, and it does not move money. Any payments to your debts are made by you, at your own bank or lender.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
