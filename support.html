How does ChipAway work?

You add your debts, then each day the app rounds up what you spent and shows how much to put towards your debts, smallest first. You make the payments yourself at your bank, and tick them off in the app.

Does ChipAway move my money?

No. ChipAway never connects to your bank and never moves money. It tells you what to pay, and you make the payment.

I'm not getting reminders

Open your iPhone's Settings, tap Notifications, choose ChipAway and make sure Allow Notifications is on. You can also change your reminder times inside the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose ChipAway and tap Cancel Subscription. Apple manages all subscriptions, so we can't cancel it for you, but you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, install ChipAway and use the Restore Purchases option in the app.

How do I delete my data?

Your debts and payments are stored on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them. See our Privacy Policy for details.
