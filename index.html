ChipAway

Chip away at your debt. Small payments. Big progress.

ChipAway is a simple iPhone app that turns your everyday spending into small extra debt payments. Each day it rounds up what you spent and shows you how to put that spare change towards your debts, smallest first, so you can watch your progress add up.
