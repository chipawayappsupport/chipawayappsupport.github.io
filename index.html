    Questions

    Does it connect to my bank?

    No. You add your spending yourself, or share a screenshot. ChipAway never asks for your bank login, account number or sort code.

    Does ChipAway move my money?

    No. It works out the extra amount to overpay and tracks your progress. You make the overpayment with your lender, as you normally would.

    Which debts can I add?

    Mortgages, loans, car finance, credit cards and store cards. Check your lender's overpayment limit and any early repayment charge first.

    Where is my information kept?

    On your phone. If you use the optional screenshot reader, the image is sent to be read and is not stored. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

    Is it available on Android?

    Not yet. ChipAway is an iPhone app for now.

    Is this financial advice?

    No. ChipAway shows estimates to help you stay motivated. It isn't financial advice.
